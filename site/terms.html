File Manager · Using the app

Terms of Use

Updated 25 October 2026 · Android package com.filegx.appsmanager

Use File Manager to manage files you own or are authorised to access. Choose permissions and file actions deliberately, and keep a separate backup of important data.

File actions and your choices

Copying, moving, renaming, sharing and deleting files can change or remove data. Check the selected files, destination and confirmation before proceeding. Permanent deletion cannot be undone by the app. Android-selected folders may support different operations from local storage. ZIP tools and recoverable Trash are limited to supported local storage.

Encrypted Vault

The Vault protects selected files with encryption based on your passphrase. There is no recovery service for a forgotten passphrase. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove private Vault and Trash contents. Export or back up important files before doing so.

Local Wi-Fi sharing

Optional Wi-Fi transfer shares the selected folder over your local network using a PIN. The connection is not encrypted. Only use a trusted network and stop the server when finished. Interrupted transfers do not resume automatically.

Optional services

Advertising and purchasing are disabled in the current release configuration. Basic local file management does not require a subscription. If optional Pro purchases become available, they are processed by Galaxy Store with the price and terms shown before payment. Restore purchases is available for supported purchases. Advertising, when enabled, must be labelled and subject to privacy choices. Development tests may use official Google demo inventory when explicitly enabled; current test inventory is off.

Availability and changes

Compatibility depends on your Android version, permissions, storage provider and device. The Galaxy Store release is in progress; a link to the store does not guarantee that the app is available in your region. Updates may change features or fix errors. A store-distributed update must come through the relevant store.

Responsible use

Do not use the app to access, copy, distribute or delete content without permission. You are responsible for the content you choose to manage or share. File Manager is a general-purpose file tool; it does not provide cloud backup or guaranteed recovery of lost data.

Privacy and support

The Privacy Policy explains local data and optional third-party services. For help, contact chennoufo11@gmail.com. Keep app and device details in the request, and omit private files and passphrases.
