Quick answers

I cannot see a folder. What should I check?

Review file access in Android Settings, or add a supported folder through Android’s folder picker. A previously granted folder can become unavailable after its permission is revoked or the storage is disconnected.

My deleted file is not in Trash.

Recoverable Trash works with supported local storage. Deletion from some Android-selected folders is permanent. The app cannot restore a permanently deleted file; check any independent backup you keep.

I forgot my Vault passphrase.

There is no recovery option or support override. Keep the passphrase safe. Do not uninstall or clear app data expecting a reset to restore encrypted files.

My computer cannot connect to Wi-Fi sharing.

Use the same trusted local network, turn on sharing in the app, and enter the exact address and PIN shown. The connection is unencrypted and may be blocked by network isolation. Stop sharing after use.

Can support recover my files?

The app has no cloud copy of your Vault or Trash. Support can help explain the tools and diagnose an issue but cannot recover a forgotten passphrase or guarantee recovery of deleted files.

Where can I find the policies?

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Both are available here and readable offline in the app.
