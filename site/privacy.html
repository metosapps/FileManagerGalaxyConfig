File Manager · Your information

Privacy Policy

Updated 25 October 2026 · Android package com.filegx.appsmanager

File Manager helps you manage files locally. It has no app account system and does not send your file contents to an analytics or advertising service.

Files and permissions

The app accesses local storage or Android-selected folders only with the permissions you grant. It reads and changes files to carry out actions you choose, such as browsing, copying, moving, renaming, ZIP creation, deletion and sharing. File names, paths, search terms and Vault contents are not ad-request parameters.

Vault, Trash and preferences

Chosen Vault files are encrypted on the device using your passphrase. The passphrase cannot be recovered. Local preferences remain until cleared or the app is removed. Vault and Trash are not cloud-backed up by this app. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove private Vault and Trash data; export important files first.

Sharing and Wi-Fi transfer

You decide which files to share or transfer. Wi-Fi transfer exposes the selected folder through a local browser connection with a PIN. The HTTP connection is not encrypted. Use a trusted network and stop sharing after use. Files transferred by your request leave the device for the recipient you choose.

Advertising, consent and purchases

Test and production advertising and purchasing are currently disabled, and unregistered Pro offers are hidden. The Galaxy build contains Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and Samsung IAP integrations for optional services. Their presence does not mean an ad request or purchase is enabled.

If advertising is enabled in a future configuration and your privacy choices permit it, Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process device, advertising and consent information. Consent is checked before ad requests, and privacy choices are offered in the app when required. Pro ownership removes ads. First-session, file-operation, Vault, permission and purchase flows contain no ad placements.

Samsung IAP processes store-account and purchase information when optional store purchases are available and used. Store terms and any displayed price apply before payment. The app does not receive your card number from the store.

Analytics and crash reporting

This build does not transmit Firebase analytics or crash reports. Analytics and crash-reporting preferences default off; no Firebase reporting backend is configured.

Configuration and this website

The app fetches signed configuration from an app-specific HTTPS endpoint hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests do not contain file names, paths or search terms. Hosting providers may process ordinary connection metadata, such as IP address, user agent and access time, to serve the website and configuration. This website uses no added analytics, advertising scripts, account system or contact form. Email support is handled through your chosen email service.

Your choices

Manage permissions and remove local app data in Android Settings. You can stop Wi-Fi sharing in the app and choose whether to use the Vault. Back up or export important data before clearing the app. Where required, advertising privacy controls are available in Privacy settings when advertising services are active.
